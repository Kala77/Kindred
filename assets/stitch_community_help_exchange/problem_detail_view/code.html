How might we improve bike safety on Elm Street during rush hour?

Since the new construction began at the intersection of Elm and 4th, cyclists are being forced into heavy traffic lanes with no buffer. The temporary detour signs are confusing for both drivers and cyclists, leading to several near-misses daily. Our community needs a better temporary solution until the bike lane is restored.
